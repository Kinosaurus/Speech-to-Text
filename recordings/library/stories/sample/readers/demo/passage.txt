Mila found a tiny silver seed beside the old gate. She carried it carefully
into the quiet garden and pressed it into the warm soil.

Soft green grass curled across the floor like a ribbon. Mila followed it as it
wound between the stones and climbed towards the hill.

White clouds drifted slowly above the warm golden hill. Their shadows moved
gently while the garden filled with the sound of a light breeze.

The little seed opened into a bright blue flower. Mila smiled, closed her book,
and promised to visit the sky garden again.
