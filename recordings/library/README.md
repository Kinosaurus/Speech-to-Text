## Notes

- Supplied story text and spoken transcripts are separate. Some corpus references are transcript-derived, not original printed text. Missing material is explicitly marked.
- Story excerpts have rebased checkpoint ranges and provisional timing. Previous live results cover complete reading tasks, not freshly tested excerpt playback.
- Only the reading interval in a full session has configured checkpoints; other tasks are not annotated as story reading.
- Shared whole-task audio uses audio.json aliases instead of duplicate WAVs. Cached resamples with identical audio have been removed.
- transcript_history/ preserves earlier ASR versions; reviews/timestamp_check/ contains the two audio reviews.
- evaluation/ holds reports; jobs/ holds current benchmark definitions; migration.json resolves historical paths. provenance/ preserves unique legacy metadata.
- New microphone captures use captures/<session>/audio.wav and transcript.srt; file-run subtitles use the recording folder asr/ directory. Temporary conversions use .cache/.
- Original eight public recordings and the supplied sample demo are tracked in Git. OCSC audio/transcripts remain ignored; follow TalkBank reuse rules and cite Wagner et al. (2025), The Ohio Child Speech Corpus.
