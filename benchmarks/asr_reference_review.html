Per-recording WER

Each recording's scores become available when its transcript is approved. Full-corpus WER requires all three reviewed recordings and complete recognition runs.

How the comparison works
  1. Every model is compared with the same reviewed spoken reference on the recordings all models completed. Failed trials remain visible separately.
  2. WER = substitutions + deletions + insertions, divided by normalized reference tokens. Case and punctuation are ignored; contractions use the benchmark’s fixed normalization.
  3. Word delay = the ASR callback arrival time minus the reviewed acoustic word ending. The scorer aligns text after the run. It uses no tracker, fuzzy matching or cue rules.
  4. Repeated words with multiple equally good alignments are excluded from timing, not guessed. Coverage shows how many words have a usable timing result.
  5. Stable timing starts when a correct word stays matched through the final snapshot. “Same-word median” uses only the exact word IDs timed correctly by every configuration. P95 requires at least 20 observations.

Timing uses your declared boundary uncertainty. First text after file start and input-delivery backlog are different measurements from word recognition delay.
